Our Story

Malli means Jasmine —
The Bloom of the Night

Malli is more than a restaurant — it is a sensory odyssey where the elegance of flowers meets the richness of Indian heritage. Each of our five private dining rooms is named after a flower that inspires its design, its ambiance, and its bespoke tasting journey.

From the fragrant warmth of Jasmine to the bold grandeur of Dahlia, every petal unfolds a new chapter of flavour, crafted by our master chefs with ingredients sourced from the finest terroirs.

The Room of Elegance

Inspired by the graceful tulip, this room embodies refined elegance with warm clay tones and champagne gold accents. The curved archways mirror the flower's gentle petals, creating an intimate setting for discerning palates.
